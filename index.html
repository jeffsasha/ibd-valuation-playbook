01 / THE ROLE

What an IBD team actually does

“Investment banking” is not investing the bank’s own money. It is advice and execution: helping corporate, sponsor, and government clients make high-stakes financing and transaction decisions.

Mergers & acquisitions

Advise a buyer or seller on valuation, deal structure, negotiation, diligence, and process strategy.

OUTPUT → PITCHBOOK, VALUATION, PROCESS MATERIALS

Equity capital markets

Help companies raise equity through IPOs, follow-ons, or private placements while framing the investor story.

OUTPUT → OFFERING ANALYSIS, INVESTOR MATERIALS

Debt & leveraged finance

Design and arrange loans or bonds, balancing borrowing capacity, cost, covenants, and rating impact.

OUTPUT → DEBT CAPACITY, TERMS, CREDIT METRICS

Restructuring & special situations

Advise companies or creditors when the capital structure is stressed and outcomes depend on liquidity and recovery.

OUTPUT → LIQUIDITY, RECOVERY, SCENARIO ANALYSIS
02 / THE PROCESS

From pitch to closing dinner

Every deal differs, but most advisory mandates move through a recognizable sequence. Analysts maintain the model, materials, data room, and process details at every step.

WEEKS 0–2

Pitch & mandate

Diagnose the client’s objective and win the engagement.

WEEKS 2–6

Preparation

Build valuation, positioning, diligence, and materials.

WEEKS 6–10

Marketing

Contact buyers or investors and distribute information.

WEEKS 10–16

Bids & diligence

Evaluate proposals and coordinate management access.

WEEKS 16–22

Negotiation

Refine price, structure, financing, and key protections.

MONTHS 6+

Sign & close

Execute agreements, satisfy conditions, transfer funds.

05 / INTERVIEW LENS

Explain the “why,” not just the formula

A strong analyst can build the model. A strong candidate can also explain which model fits the question, where it can fail, and how its assumptions connect to the business.

Technical questions to rehearse

EV / EBITDA is capital-structure neutral: enterprise value belongs to all capital providers, and EBITDA is before interest. P / E compares equity value with earnings after interest, so leverage and tax differences can distort comparisons.
A buyer usually pays for control and may share some expected synergies with the seller. Precedents can also reflect different market conditions, which is why transaction selection and timing matter.
Value falls because future cash flows are discounted more heavily. Terminal value also declines under the Gordon Growth method because WACC appears in the denominator as WACC minus terminal growth.
Yes. EPS accretion is an accounting outcome, not proof of positive net present value. A buyer can overpay, use cheap debt, and still show near-term EPS accretion while earning below its cost of capital.
Start with enterprise value, subtract debt and debt-like claims, subtract preferred stock and noncontrolling interests, then add cash and non-operating investments. The exact bridge depends on what the operating metric includes.

An analyst’s workstream

ANALYZE

Own the numbers

Update historicals, operating forecasts, valuation, merger consequences, and scenario cases.

SYNTHESIZE

Make the message land

Turn analysis into clear slides: the answer, the evidence, the implication, and the action.

COORDINATE

Keep the process moving

Track diligence, buyer questions, approvals, comments, and the latest version of every work product.

VERIFY

Protect accuracy

Check formulas, sources, units, dates, footnotes, and consistency before work reaches a client.

06 / QUICK REFERENCE

The language of valuation

These terms recur across nearly every interview, model, and deal discussion.

ENTERPRISE VALUE

Value of core operations to all capital providers.

EQUITY VALUE

Value attributable to common shareholders.

WACC

Blended required return of debt and equity investors.

UNLEVERED FCF

Cash flow before debt payments, available to all investors.

CONTROL PREMIUM

Extra value paid to obtain decision-making control.

SYNERGY

Incremental value from combining businesses.

ACCRETION

Increase in the buyer’s pro forma EPS after a deal.

IRR

Annualized return that sets investment NPV to zero.

Valuation is a range, not a verdict.

The real skill is connecting assumptions to the business, triangulating methods, and communicating what changes the answer.
